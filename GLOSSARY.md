# Songhealth

The public website and CMS of สำนักงานสาธารณสุขอำเภอสอง (the Song District Public Health Office): what
citizens find, and what staff publish. Detailed meaning lives in
`docs/website-modernization-docs/current/00`–`09`; this file fixes the words.

## Content kinds

**News** (ข่าว):
A dated public-relations story from the office or its network.
_Avoid_: Post, article

**Health News** (ข่าวสุขภาพ):
News about a Health Topic. A view of News, not a separate kind.
_Avoid_: Health article

**Activity** (กิจกรรม):
A record of a meeting, field visit or event the office or its network took part in.
_Avoid_: Event post, gallery

**Announcement** (ประกาศ):
Official information people must notice or act on, possibly with its own open and close dates.
_Avoid_: Notice post

**Health Content** (ความรู้สุขภาพ):
Evergreen public-health knowledge for citizens.
_Avoid_: Health News, health article

**Health Report** (รายงานสุขภาพ):
A described report whose authoritative content is a Document.
_Avoid_: Health metric, dataset

**Document** (เอกสารเผยแพร่):
A public record about one authoritative published file, such as a form, guide, plan or report.
_Avoid_: File, attachment, media

**Basic Page** (หน้าเนื้อหาพื้นฐาน):
Standalone prose that fits no structured kind, such as a privacy notice.
_Avoid_: Page (alone), static page

## Services and facilities

**Service** (บริการประชาชน):
A citizen-facing service or procedure that the office provides, coordinates or points to, described as guidance.
_Avoid_: Using it for forms or guides (Documents) or for finding facilities (Health Service Directory)

**Health Service** (หน่วยบริการสุขภาพที่เกี่ยวข้อง):
A real health facility related to the district, such as a hospital or รพ.สต., under its own affiliation.
_Avoid_: หน่วยบริการในพื้นที่, หน่วยบริการใกล้บ้าน, หน่วยบริการในสังกัด

**Health Service Directory** (ค้นหาหน่วยบริการ):
The public way to find Health Services by area or name.
_Avoid_: Treating it as a Service

**Area** (พื้นที่):
An official administrative area, from province down to village, identified by its official code.
_Avoid_: Zone, region

**Health Topic** (ประเด็นสุขภาพ):
A shared classification of health subject matter used to group content.
_Avoid_: Category (for this meaning)

## People and the office

**Personnel** (บุคลากร):
A public directory record of a staff member's current public position and unit.
_Avoid_: Staff account, user, employee record

**Leadership** (ผู้บริหาร):
The Personnel who hold the office's executive positions, in order of rank. A view of Personnel, not a separate kind.
_Avoid_: Executive profile, management type

**Office Contact** (ข้อมูลติดต่อสำนักงาน):
The office-wide address, telephone, opening hours and official channels that citizens use to reach the office.
_Avoid_: Contact entity, contact record

## Presentation

**Homepage** (หน้าแรก):
A curated and automated view of existing content. It owns no content of its own.
_Avoid_: Landing content, homepage posts

**Featured** (เรื่องเด่น):
Existing published content chosen to headline the Homepage. A presentation choice, not a content kind.
_Avoid_: Banner, hero post, featured type

**Frequently Used Services** (บริการที่ใช้บ่อย):
The Services that staff pick to show on the Homepage for common citizen tasks.
_Avoid_: Popular services

**Quick Access** (เข้าถึงข้อมูล):
A staff-maintained set of shortcuts to existing destinations. It deliberately repeats navigation.
_Avoid_: Quick Links section, second sitemap
